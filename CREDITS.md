# Credits

## Piece artwork
The jungle animals on the pieces (gorilla, tiger head, rhinoceros, rattlesnake,
frog, ant) come from [Game Icons](https://game-icons.net) by Lorc, Delapouite
and contributors, licensed under
[CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/). We place them on
round tokens and recolor them for the day and night jungles.

## Openings
Opening names come from the [Lichess chess-openings](https://github.com/lichess-org/chess-openings)
dataset (public domain, CC0).

## Engine
[Stockfish](https://stockfishchess.org) (GPLv3), via the `stockfish` npm package.
